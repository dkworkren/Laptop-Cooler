## Background
My laptop has been crashing a lot recently and I have a feeling that it is because of overheating. My screen also likes to suddenly turn purple and pixelized which I suspect is from a faulty GPU. Laptop coolers in the market are just too tall for my usage and too large (since they are mostly designed for gaming laptops). Anyways I've decided to make a laptop cooler from scratch in hopes of cooling my laptop while I do CAD, Programming or other robotics related stuff.

## How it Works
4 DC Motors and 4 propellers attached to it that are located right beneath the laptop turns on, sucks surrounding air and redirects them to the laptop. The stand itself is also made so air can flow around the laptop itself.

## How to Use
Just plug in a USB-C cable to the cooler and it will turn on automatically, to turn it off please gently unplug the cable from the cooler.
